# Analytics

Analytics should answer whether ParentPilot reliably reduces missed work and review effort—not profile children or inspect school content.

**Proposed product signals:** onboarding/upload funnel, processing outcome, candidate reviewed/accepted/edited/rejected, conflict/override reviewed, calendar/reminder engagement, correction rate, time-to-review, and deletion/export completion. AI quality is measured primarily through governed evaluation, not inferred solely from clicks.

Use coarse, documented event schemas with pseudonymous account IDs and non-sensitive categories. Never send names, email addresses, school names, document text, event titles, dates/times, source excerpts, or raw errors. Minimize properties, define retention, restrict access, and honor consent/opt-out requirements.

Analytics provider, consent model, event taxonomy, retention, aggregation thresholds, and success targets are **TBD**. No advertising, sale of data, or child profiling is in scope.
