# Roadmap

Sequence indicates dependency, not dates or delivery estimates.

## Foundation

- Confirm product rules, trust model, privacy posture, and MVP boundaries
- Select backend/auth/storage approach and email-ingestion model
- Establish iOS architecture, environments, CI, testing, and observability
- Define evaluation dataset and release gates using synthetic/de-identified fixtures

## MVP

- Authentication and multi-child/multi-school setup
- PDF upload; school email ingestion
- Asynchronous extraction pipeline and review experience
- Calendar, reminders, recurring events, and dated overrides
- Provenance, duplicate/change/conflict handling, deletion/export basics
- Security, accessibility, localization readiness, and operational runbooks

## Post-MVP

- Improve extraction from evaluation and corrections
- Share extension for user-shared WhatsApp content
- Screenshot/image ingestion
- Richer notification controls and family collaboration (**TBD**)
- Offline/read caching and broader calendar interoperability (**TBD**)

## Future integrations

- Public school websites
- School portals only through authorized, supportable integration patterns
- Official school APIs

Portal scraping is explicitly outside MVP. Each integration requires a privacy, reliability, authorization, and source-trust review.
